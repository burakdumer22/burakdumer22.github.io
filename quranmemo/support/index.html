Common questions

Is QuranMemo free? Yes — everything you need to memorize is free, forever. The optional one-time "Support QuranMemo" upgrade adds themes, full-history stats, and backup.

Does it work offline? Yes, fully. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

Does the app check my recitation? No. QuranMemo is an honest self-check tool: hide, recite, reveal, and grade yourself — you can also record and listen back to your own recitation. It does not evaluate your recitation and is not a substitute for a teacher.

Where does the Quran text come from? The Tanzil Project (Uthmani edition), used verbatim with attribution and verified cryptographically at every launch.
